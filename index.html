Data & measurement

Map and building footprints: OpenStreetMap / OpenFreeMap. Heights depend on available data; missing heights use an 8 m display fallback. These are extruded building volumes.

Direct distance uses the great-circle distance between road-snapped endpoints. Road distance sums the routed driving path; OSRM selects a fast legal driving route, which is not guaranteed to be the shortest distance. Driving distances can differ by direction.

Route time is OSRM’s model estimate, not live traffic. The congestion control below is simulated. Public map and routing services require an internet connection.
